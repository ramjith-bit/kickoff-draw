# Catalog and ratings — 24 October 2026

The catalog contains 839 unique EA team IDs: 762 club entries, 60 men's national teams and 17 women's national teams from FIFA Index's published FC 26 database. The global club list and individual league lists were reconciled by EA team ID because the global overall-sorted pagination repeats some teams at ties. Count checks were made against league pages; Recoleta FC, Finland and South Africa were checked individually to resolve omissions at pagination boundaries.

- FC 26 team list: https://fifaindex.com/teams/fc26
- FC 26 leagues: https://fifaindex.com/leagues/fc26
- FC 26 men: https://fifaindex.com/teams/fc26?category=men
- FC 26 women: https://fifaindex.com/teams/fc26?category=women
- FC 27 team ratings: https://fifaindex.com/teams
- FC 27 leagues: https://fifaindex.com/leagues
- National-team ratings are also taken from the FC 27 International and International Women listings.

739 FC 26 entries match a published FC 27 rating by the same EA team ID. 100 unmatched entries retain their FC 26 values and are labeled **FC 26 fallback** on cards and in the team list. The “FC 27 ratings only” setting excludes them. No FC 27 values are inferred or invented for removed, unlisted or changed-ID teams.

These database ratings are a reference for a user-supplied FC 27 squad mod inside FC 26. They have not been extracted from that installed mod. Mod-specific team availability, custom clubs and rating changes can differ. Names and competitions remain from FC 26; overall, attack, midfield and defence come from the matched rating snapshot. Manually editing a rating marks it Custom.

Original crest artwork uses the image URLs actually displayed on the source pages, under `https://images.fifaindex.com/fc26/teams/` or `/fc27/teams/`. Crests remain the property of their respective teams. This is an independent fan companion; no EA, club or data-provider affiliation is implied. An image-failure fallback uses initials. Crests require a connection on first view and are cached for later offline use.

The complete transformed catalog is in `team-catalog.json`. Its team IDs are stable across releases. Saved preferences, exclusions, history and user edits migrate from the original 32-team app. Legacy custom teams with only a star rating remain saved; enter an overall rating to include them in the new overall-based draw.
