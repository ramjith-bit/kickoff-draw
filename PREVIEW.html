About the team data

The starter pool contains 16 men’s and 16 women’s clubs from the FC 26 star-rating reveal reported by Sports Illustrated on 6 September 2025 (updated 30 September). It is not the complete database and may differ from your installed squad update.

Source: si.com/soccer/32-highest-rated-teams-ea-sports-fc-26-revealed

National teams and lower-rated clubs can be added here. Balanced draws use star ratings; no numeric overall ratings are implied. Team badges are generated initials, not official crests.

Independent fan-made companion. Not affiliated with EA SPORTS.
